Why a blockchain

Copy-proof claims.

  1. 01

    Commit

    Send only hash(route, salt, you). Nobody can read or reuse your answer.

  2. 02

    Reveal

    One block later, reveal the route. The hash is bound to your wallet, so a copy from the mempool fails.

  3. 03

    Paid

    The contract replays every move. Valid? The escrowed AVAX goes straight to you.
